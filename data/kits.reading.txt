In the first module, students will engineer their own "Hot Packs" and "Cold Packs." By dissolving specific salts in water, they will observe how breaking and forming chemical bonds can either release massive amounts of heat (Exothermic) or absorb energy to freeze water (Endothermic). In the second module, students will visualize the invisible movement of electrons. Using an iron nail and copper sulfate solution, they will witness a Single Displacement Reaction, identifying which elements are oxidized and which are reduced, providing a tangible look at the chemistry behind batteries and corrosion.
