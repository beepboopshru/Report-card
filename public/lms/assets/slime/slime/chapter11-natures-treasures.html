Nature\'s Treasures

Key Concepts

"Air, water, soil, sunlight, plants and animals — these are Nature\'s treasures. Without them, life on Earth would not exist."
Big Idea

Nature\'s Gifts

Around us, every day, nature provides:

  • 🌬️ Air — to breathe
  • 💧 Water — to drink, cook, clean
  • 🌍 Soil — to grow food
  • ☀️ Sunlight — for energy & life
  • 🌳 Plants & animals — for food, materials
All living beings depend on these treasures. We must protect them.
11.1

Air — A Mix of Gases

Air is invisible but essential. Hold your breath for 30 sec — you\'ll understand!

Composition:

  • 78% Nitrogen
  • 21% Oxygen
  • 1% Argon + CO₂ + others
We can survive days without food, but only minutes without oxygen!
Wind

Wind = Moving Air

Air on the move = wind. You see it in:

  • Leaves rustling
  • Clothes swaying
  • Firki (paper pinwheel) rotating
  • Windmills generating power!
India has big wind farms: Muppandal (Tamil Nadu), Jaisalmer (Rajasthan), Brahmanvel (Maharashtra) — clean energy from wind!
11.2

Water — Precious Treasure

2/3 of Earth is water — but:

  • Most is SALTY ocean water (~97%)
  • 2% locked in ice
  • Only ~1% is fresh, accessible drinking water!
⚠️ Don\'t waste water. Turn off taps. Fix leaks. Reuse where possible.
Sources

Where Does Water Come From?

  • Rivers & lakes
  • Underground (wells, borewells)
  • Rain
  • Glaciers melting

All connected by the water cycle — evaporation, condensation, rain.

11.3

Soil — Earth\'s Skin

The dark layer on top of land — where plants grow.

Made from:

  • Tiny rock particles
  • Decayed plants & animals (humus)
  • Water + air in spaces
  • Microorganisms
Without soil — no farming, no food. Save soil!
Soil types

11.4

Sunlight — Energy from the Sky

Sun provides:

  • Light to see
  • Heat to warm
  • Energy for plants (photosynthesis)
  • Source of Vitamin D for us!
  • Drives the water cycle
Without the Sun — Earth would be a frozen, dark, lifeless ball.
Solar power

Solar Energy

We can convert sunlight directly to electricity using solar panels.

India is a world leader in solar power — especially with the International Solar Alliance (HQ in Gurugram).

  • Solar lights in remote villages
  • Solar water heaters
  • Solar cookers
  • Big solar farms
11.5

Plants & Animals — Living Treasures

Plants give us:

  • Oxygen 🌬️
  • Food (fruit, veg, grains)
  • Wood, paper, medicines
  • Shelter for animals

Animals give us:

  • Milk, eggs, honey
  • Wool, leather, silk
  • Help in farming (bulls, cows)
  • Pollination (bees!)
11.6

Save the Treasures!

Air pollution, water pollution, deforestation, soil erosion — all human-made problems.

Easy ways to help:

  • Use less plastic 🥤
  • Plant trees 🌳
  • Turn off taps 💧
  • Walk / cycle / public transport 🚲
  • Reduce, Reuse, Recycle ♻️
India

🇮🇳 Indian Conservation

India has many great traditions of conserving nature:

  • Sacred groves — forests preserved for centuries by communities
  • Bishnoi community in Rajasthan — protect blackbucks & trees
  • Chipko movement — Himalayan villagers hugging trees to prevent cutting
  • "Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam" — the world is one family

📖 Swara & Ahana visit Ahana\'s Ajji (Grandmother)

Summer vacation. The two friends visit Ahana\'s ajji in a village near the Western Ghats. Cool air. Birds chirping. A stream nearby.

Swara: Wow! The air feels so... clean!

Ajji: Yes! Pure mountain air. You know — it\'s not just empty space. Air is a mix of gases. 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, 1% others.

Ahana: Why do we need air?

Ajji: Our bodies need oxygen for everything — breathing, energy, even thinking. Hold your breath for 30 seconds and see how uncomfortable it feels!

A breeze blows. Leaves rustle. The girls see a firki spin nearby.

Ahana: The wind moves things!

Ajji: Moving air = wind. India uses wind to make electricity — huge windmill farms in Tamil Nadu and Rajasthan.

Swara: What\'s in the stream? Why is the water so clear?

Ajji: Fresh water from melting snow up in the hills. Freshwater is precious — only 1% of Earth\'s water is fresh and usable. The rest is salty oceans!

Ahana: So we have to save it...

Ajji: Yes — every drop counts. Turn off taps, fix leaks, don\'t waste.

She bends down, picks up some soil.

Ajji: See this soil? It\'s made of tiny rocks, decayed plants and animals (called humus), water, air, and tiny living things. Without soil — no food, no trees, no us!

Swara: And the sun?

Ajji: Sunlight gives us light, warmth, vitamin D, and powers all plant growth. We can even use solar panels to make electricity!

Ahana: So many treasures from nature...

Ajji: Yes, beta. Air, water, soil, sun, plants, animals — without them, there\'s no life. Our job is to PROTECT them.

🧪 Lab Experiments

Activity 11.1 — Breath-Holding Easy

Procedure

  1. Take a deep breath in.
  2. Hold as long as you can.
  3. Note how you feel — uncomfortable!
  4. That\'s because your body NEEDS oxygen.

Activity 11.2 — Make a Firki Easy

Materials

  • Square paper (15cm)
  • Scissors
  • Pin
  • Soft stick

Procedure

  1. Fold paper diagonally, cut from each corner toward centre (not all the way).
  2. Bring alternate corners to centre.
  3. Pin through them, attach to stick.
  4. Run with it or blow — watch it spin!

Activity 11.3 — Water Audit Easy

Procedure

  1. For one day, list every water use in your home (handwash, dishes, bathing...).
  2. Note how it\'s wasted (taps left running, leaks).
  3. Suggest 1 fix per case.
  4. Discuss with family.

Activity 11.4 — Soil Test Easy

Materials

  • Sandy soil, clay, garden soil samples
  • 3 funnels + 3 jars
  • Cotton (to plug holes)
  • Water

Procedure

  1. Cotton at bottom of each funnel. Add equal soil.
  2. Pour same amount of water on each.
  3. Time how long water takes to drip through.
  4. Sandy drains fast. Clay slow. Loamy medium.
