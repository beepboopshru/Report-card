Mindful Eating: A Path to a Healthy Body

Key Concepts

"Who is healthy? One who eats food that is wholesome, in moderate quantities, and appropriate for the season, time and place."
— Sanskrit Subhāṣita (wise saying)
Big Idea

Food = Life

annena jātāni jīvanti — "Food gives life to living beings."

Our bodies need food daily to grow, repair, get energy, and stay protected from diseases.

"Health is the ultimate wealth" — eat well and you live well.
3.1

Diversity in Food

India is huge — different states eat very different foods, based on what grows there.

  • Punjab: makki di roti, sarson da saag, chhole, lassi
  • Karnataka: idli, dosa, ragi mudde, sambhar
  • Manipur: rice, eromba, singju, kangsoi
  • Kerala: appam, fish curry, coconut chutney
Climate + crops + culture = each state's unique cuisine.

3.2

Components of Food

Our food has 5 main nutrients + water + dietary fibre:

  • 🍞 Carbohydrates — energy
  • 🥜 Fats — stored energy
  • 🥚 Proteins — growth & repair
  • 🍊 Vitamins — protect body
  • 🥗 Minerals — many functions
  • 💧 Water — every cell needs it
  • 🌾 Dietary fibre — for digestion
Energy

Carbohydrates & Fats — Energy Givers

Carbohydrates = quick energy:

  • Rice, wheat, potato, maize, jowar, ragi, bajra

Fats = stored energy (keeps us warm in winter):

  • Butter, ghee, oils, nuts (almonds, walnuts), seeds, paneer, cheese
Polar bears eat lots of fat — they hibernate on it for months!
Build

Proteins — Body Builders

Help your body GROW and REPAIR. Important for kids and sportspersons.

  • 🌱 Plant sources: pulses, beans, peas, soybean, nuts
  • 🥚 Animal sources: milk, paneer, eggs, fish, meat
  • 🍄 Edible mushrooms are also rich in protein!
Protect

Vitamins & Minerals — Protectors

Needed in SMALL amounts but essential to keep diseases away.

VitaminSourceDeficiency disease
APapaya, carrot, mango, milkNight blindness

Minerals

Key Minerals

MineralUseLack causes
CalciumBones & teethWeak bones, tooth decay
IodineMental + physical activityGoitre
IronBlood (haemoglobin)Anaemia (weakness)
India fortifies common salt with iodine to prevent goitre — especially in regions with low iodine in soil.
Fibre & Water

Dietary Fibre & Water

Dietary fibre (roughage) — doesn't give nutrients but helps digestion. Sources: green leafy veggies, fruits, whole grains, pulses, nuts.

Water — every cell needs it. Helps digestion, removes wastes, controls body temperature.

Drink 6-8 glasses of water a day!
Balanced Diet

A Balanced Plate

A meal that has the right proportions of ALL nutrients = a balanced diet.

  • 🍚 Half plate of carbs (rice/roti)
  • 🥗 Quarter plate of veggies
  • 🥚 Quarter plate of protein (dal/curd/egg)
  • 🍎 Fruit on the side
  • 💧 Water!
India

Salt & the Agariyas

The Agariya tribe in the Little Rann of Kutch, Gujarat, harvests salt from seawater. For 8 months a year, they live in the desert doing this hard work.

The iodised salt in your kitchen often comes from these salt farms!
Cooking smart

Don't Lose the Nutrients!

Watch out — cooking can destroy some nutrients:

  • Vitamin C is destroyed by high heat → eat raw fruits
  • Washing cut vegetables loses vitamins
  • Frying adds extra fat — bake/grill when possible
  • Always wash BEFORE cutting

🎯 ENGAGE

📖 Tiffin Time — Swara, Ahana & Amma

Lunch break at school. Swara and Ahana open their tiffins. Ahana's amma packed today\'s tiffin — and dropped off lunch boxes for both girls.

Swara: Wah! Roti, sabzi, curd, salad, AND a banana!

Ahana: Amma calls this a balanced diet. She says every meal should have something from each group.

Swara: Each group? Like what?

Ahana: Roti and rice give us carbohydrates — for energy. Like petrol for the body.

Swara: And the dal?

Ahana: Dal has proteins — to grow and repair the body. That\'s why athletes eat lots of pulses, eggs, and milk.

Amma walks by during recess, brings extra fruit for them both.

Ahana\'s Amma: Don\'t forget your guava! It\'s full of vitamin C. Without it, you can get a disease called scurvy — bleeding gums.

Swara: Mom told me about sailors who got scurvy on long sea voyages, no lemons!

Amma: Exactly. And the salt in your sabzi? It\'s iodised — iodine helps prevent goitre, swelling at the neck. Indian salt is fortified with iodine since the 1960s.

Ahana: What about ghee, almonds, butter?

Amma: Those are fats — stored energy. Polar bears live for months on theirs!

Swara: And the salad?

Amma: Loaded with vitamins, minerals, and dietary fibre. Fibre doesn\'t give nutrients — but it helps digestion. Without it, you feel uncomfortable.

Ahana: And water?

Amma: EVERY part of your body needs water. 6-8 glasses a day, minimum.

Swara: (slowly looking at her tiffin) So this one tiffin has carbs, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, water AND fibre... seven food components!

Amma: A balanced diet! That\'s why eating from one group only — like only chips, or only fruits — won\'t make you strong. You need all of them.

🔬 EXPLORE

Try it yourself.

🍯 Experiment 1: Iodine Test for Starch

Drop iodine solution on different foods (rice, bread, banana, apple). Foods with starch (a carbohydrate) turn BLUE-BLACK. Others don\'t.

🍞 Experiment 2: Test for Fats

Take a small piece of food (peanut, butter, paneer). Rub on a piece of paper. Hold up to light — a translucent (oily) spot = fat!

📋 Experiment 3: Track Your Week\'s Food

For 7 days, write down everything you eat. End of week — circle items rich in each nutrient. Are you eating balanced?

📖 EXPLAIN

Key insight: Each nutrient does a different job. You need ALL of them, every day, in small amounts. That\'s a balanced diet.

🧪 Lab Experiments

Textbook activities + bonus add-ons.

Activity 3.1 — Track Your Weekly Diet Easy

Materials

  • Notebook + pen
  • One week

Procedure

  1. Each day, write all the foods you ate.
  2. End of week: compare with friends.
  3. Which foods did you eat the most?
  4. Are any nutrient groups missing?

Activity 3.2 — Cuisine Map of India Easy

Procedure

  1. Pick 5 Indian states.
  2. Find traditional foods for each (ask family, books, internet).
  3. Match crops grown in that region with the dishes.
  4. Make a poster or table.

Activity 3.3 — Then-and-Now Cooking Easy

Procedure

  1. Interview grandparents about how cooking was done in their childhood.
  2. List 5 things that changed (chulha → gas, etc).
  3. Try one traditional method — like making chapati on chulha (with adult).

Activity 3.4 — Survey Deficiency Diseases Medium

Procedure

  1. Study the deficiency disease chart.
  2. In your neighbourhood, do you spot anyone with symptoms?
  3. Suggest dietary changes (NOT replace medical advice!)
  4. Always suggest they see a doctor.

Add-On 1 — Starch Test at Home Adult Help

Materials

  • Iodine solution (from chemist)
  • Dropper
  • Various foods: rice (cooked), banana, apple, butter, paneer
  • White plate

Procedure

  1. Put a small piece of each food on the plate.
  2. Drop iodine on each.
  3. Foods with STARCH turn dark blue/black. Others stay yellow/brown.

Safety

Iodine stains! Don\'t eat the tested foods. Wash hands.

Add-On 2 — Make Iodised Salt Awareness Poster Easy

Procedure

  1. Find an iodised salt packet — read the label.
  2. Find a non-iodised salt — compare.
  3. Research: why is iodine added?
  4. Make a poster for your school explaining the importance of iodised salt.
