Big Idea

What IS Science?

Science is not a stack of facts. It is an adventure:

  • We ask questions
  • We explore the world
  • We try to understand how things work

The most important thing? Curiosity! That's why your textbook is called Curiosity.

Everywhere!

Science is Everywhere

Science is happening:

  • In the night sky 🌌 — why do stars shine?
  • In your garden 🌸 — how does a flower know when to bloom?
  • In the kitchen 🍳 — why does dal spill out of the cooker?
  • In the rain ☔ — why does it rain at all?
Even tiny grains of sand have stories — and so do massive mountains!
Puzzle

A Giant, Endless Puzzle

Science is like a jigsaw puzzle 🧩 — but one that never ends.

Every new discovery adds another piece. Sometimes we find a piece was in the wrong spot — and we have to move it!

🌍 Earth 🌱 Plants 💧 Water 🌟 Stars 🍎 Food ⚡ Energy ?
Together

Science is a Team Sport

Scientists across the world work together — often in big teams.

So if you can't find an answer yourself, ask your friends, your teacher, your family. Discovering together is more fun!

And remember — you won't find every answer in Grade 6. You're starting a journey that lasts for the next five years (and beyond!).

🔬 The Scientific Method — 5 Easy Steps

Whether it's a scientist in a lab, your mother in the kitchen, a bicycle repair person, or an electrician — they all follow the same steps to figure things out. Click any step below for details!

1
👀

OBSERVE

Look around. Notice something interesting or puzzling.

2
❓

WONDER

Ask a question. "Why is this happening?"

3
💡

GUESS

Think of a possible answer. This is called a "hypothesis".

4
🧪

TEST

Do an experiment or look more carefully. Is your guess right?

5
📊

ANALYSE

Look at the results. Did your guess work? If not — guess again!

Click a step above to learn more

The 5 steps fit together. Sometimes you go forward. Sometimes you loop back. That's totally fine — that's how science actually works!

"Science is not just about memorising facts. It is about following a step-by-step process to find answers."

📖 Story & Cartoons

A First Day Like No Other

Meet Diya and Veer. They're starting Grade 6 — and Science as a new subject. Let's see what happens when their curiosity catches fire.

1. First day at school. Diya and Veer get a brand-new textbook with the word "Curiosity" on it. "Why curiosity?" wonders Veer.
2. On the way home, Diya's pen suddenly stops writing. "What?! Why??" That single question begins their adventure.
3. STEP 1 — They OBSERVE. The pen looks fine from outside. STEP 2 — They WONDER. "Could the ink be over?" STEP 3 — they GUESS.
4. STEP 4 — TEST! They open the pen, check the ink refill. It's full! New guess: maybe the ink dried up. They test again with a tissue.
5. STEP 5 — ANALYSE. They wiped the nib, ink flows again! Mystery solved. "Wait... did we just do SCIENCE?" "We did!"
6. Diya: "So science isn't just in the lab?" Veer: "It's EVERYWHERE — in the kitchen, the bike repair shop, even when we're fixing our pens!"
"When we try to ask questions and find out answers — we are all, in a way, scientists!"
— Grade 6 Curiosity
